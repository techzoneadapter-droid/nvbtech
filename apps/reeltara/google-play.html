India – App description

ReelTara helps you discover great films. Explore movie details, enjoy available official trailers, save your watchlist, and find legitimate streaming services for India. Stream selected freely licensed films only when the original source allows playback.

Philippines – App description

Explore movies with ReelTara. Browse film information, watch available trailers, save favourites, and find official streaming services available in the Philippines. Selected films licensed for free playback may be watched inside ReelTara.

Legal movie sources

Commercial movies are not streamed in full in ReelTara without distribution rights. The app only provides information, available trailers and links to lawful services for those titles. Free playback is subject to the video source, content licence and regional restrictions.
